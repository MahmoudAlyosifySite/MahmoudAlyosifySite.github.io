About Me

I'm an AI & Data Science Engineer with a Bachelor's in Computer & Information Science (Bioinformatics) from Assiut University and currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Queen's University, Canada through the The Presidential Scholarship — a competitive national AI graduate fellowship.

My research and engineering work spans LLM fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), self-supervised contrastive learning, deep reinforcement learning for cloud optimization, and applied predictive ML for space safety. I deploy production-oriented AI systems on AWS and integrate RAG pipelines, multi-agent workflows, and ONNX/FAISS for real-world performance.
