1. Types of Gradient Descent

The core difference between optimization algorithms lies in data strategy: how much data is used to calculate the gradient step? This section visualizes the specific "paths" described in Lecture 3. Notice how Batch GD moves directly to the center, while Stochastic GD creates a chaotic "zig-zag" pattern, and Mini-batch offers a balanced, slightly wavy approach.

Simulation Controls

Toggle paths to compare their behavior on the cost landscape.

Key Takeaway

Mini-batch is the standard for a reason. It allows GPU parallelization (like Batch) but updates weights frequently enough to converge faster than Batch (like SGD).
